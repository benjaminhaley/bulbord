Bulbord Privacy Policy

Last updated August 7, 2026

Bulbord is an invite-only community bulletin app. This page covers what we collect, why, and who can see it. It applies to every institution community on Bulbord (Nettelhorst today; others may join the platform later).

Who can use it

Bulbord is invite-only — you need an invite link from an existing member to join, and the app is not directed at or designed for use by children. Every account belongs to an adult member of the community (a parent, guardian, or staff member), not a child.

What we collect

When you join, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and (optionally) a profile photo
  • A passkey credential (a cryptographic public key tied to your device's Face ID/Touch ID/Windows Hello/Android biometric) — we never see or store your fingerprint, face data, or PIN itself, only the public key your device uses to prove it's you
  • Which existing member invited you
  • Content you post — event/camp listings, comments, feedback, and photos you attach to them
  • Which events/camps you've marked interested in or dismissed, so we can show attendance signals like "3 interested: You, Alice and Bob" to other members

What we don't do

We don't run ads, sell your data, or share it with third parties for marketing. We don't track you across other apps or websites. Payment information, if we ever add paid bookings, is handled entirely by a payment processor (e.g. Stripe) — we never store your card details ourselves.

Who can see what

Event and camp listings are visible to every invited member of your community. Your name and photo are visible to members you've invited or who invited you (so people can see who's part of the community). More sensitive information — if we add features like babysitter contact details or booking specifics in the future — will only ever be visible to the people directly involved in that transaction, not the whole community.

Deletion and retention

If you'd like your account or content removed, email us (below) and we'll take care of it. We keep a backup history for a limited window so accidental deletions can be recovered, but nothing is shared or used beyond that.
